2. In short

  • Your calendar, reminders, notes and lists (with attached photos, files and voice notes), daily routine, goals, workouts and streak are stored only on your phone.
  • The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent to personalised ads (from age 18), and you can withdraw it at any time. Elsewhere, from age 18 you can turn personalised ads on or off in Settings → Privacy settings.
  • Planstep can be used from age 16. On first launch we ask for your date of birth (year, month, day; it stays on your phone and is never sent anywhere): under 16 the app does not start, and between 16 and 18 only non-personalised ads are shown and no consent is asked.
  • For the weather, the coordinates of your saved cities are sent to MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute).
  • The developer uses no analytics or tracking and does not sell any data.

3. Data stored on your phone

You enter this data yourself, and it stays only in the app's private storage on your phone:

  • events, reminders, recurrences and categories;
  • notes and lists with attached photos, documents and voice notes (copied into the app's own storage);
  • daily routines, goals, workout templates and finished workouts;
  • daily progress and the streak (signed against tampering);
  • saved cities, downloaded weather forecasts and hourly weather values (kept for up to 2 days) and settings (language, country, theme);
  • your date of birth (year, month, day), entered on first launch – used only to work out your age: it decides whether you can use the app (from age 16) and how ads are set up. Legal basis: legal obligations to protect minors (GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) and Art. 8). Answering is required: without it the app can't be used, because safe ad settings depend on it. If you previously entered only the year and your age group depends on your birthday, we ask once for the month and day too. Outside the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, your choice about personalised ads is also stored here;
  • if you turn them on: events copied from your phone's calendar, and names and birthdays from your contacts;
  • after a crash, a local error log that only leaves your phone if you send it by email yourself.

The developer has no access to any of this. To delete it: Settings → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.

4. Permissions – what for and when

  • Notifications: only what you set up yourself – reminders (as a notification or full screen), the end of a countdown, workout and timer alerts, and daily tips if you turn them on. By default the app sends no notifications, and it asks for the permission only when you first set one up. On the lock screen, reminder and event notifications follow the phone's setting: if hiding sensitive content is turned on, only the fact that you have a reminder is shown, not its title.
  • Exact alarms: so reminders and the countdown alert you exactly on time.
  • Full-screen notifications: so a "Full screen" reminder and the end of a countdown also appear on the lock screen (the title is shown there only if the phone's setting allows it). From Android 14, only if you allow it in settings.
  • Run at startup: reminders are restored after the phone restarts.
  • Read calendar (only if you turn it on): events from your phone's calendar are copied one way into Planstep. Nothing is written back or sent anywhere.
  • Read contacts (only if you turn it on): only names and birthdays, for birthday reminders.
  • Microphone (only while you use it): recording a voice note for a note (the recording stays on your phone) and dictation.
  • Approximate location (only when you tap "Current location"): to find your city. Precise location is never requested, and never in the background.
  • Internet: ads, weather, city search.
  • Photos and files: the app uses Android's own photo picker and file picker; it only gets access to the item you select, never to your whole gallery.

5. What leaves your phone, and where to?

Google AdMob and User Messaging Platform (Google Ireland Limited, Google LLC)

Purpose: showing and measuring ads, and managing consent. Data processed: advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (model, Android version, language), interactions with ads and your consent status. Google processes this under its own policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads and for the use of identifiers stored on your device (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)); legitimate interest for non-personalised ads and for preventing fraud and abuse (Art. 6(1)(f)). Residents of certain US states can opt out of the sharing of their ad data in the same place where consent is managed (see section 9).

If the date of birth you entered means you are under 18, ad requests tell Google so (with child-directed treatment), turn personalisation off and ask for non-personalised ads: then there are no personalised ads or remarketing, the advertising ID is not transmitted, and no consent is asked. The date of birth itself is never sent to anyone. Outside the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, from age 18 you decide with the Settings → Privacy settings → Personalized ads switch: when it is off, ad requests ask for non-personalised ads and signal restricted data processing. If you turn 18 while using the app, we ask you on the next launch whether you want personalised ads (in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks this). The number of ads does not depend on your choice.

MET Norway (Meteorologisk institutt, Norway)

Purpose: weather forecast (sunrise, sunset and the moon phase are calculated on your phone). Data sent: the coordinates of the saved city (rounded to 2 decimal places, about 1 kilometre), the app's name and version with the contact email address, and – as with any internet request – your IP address. The requests reach MET Norway through the caching service of BunnyWay d.o.o. (Slovenia, EU; bunny.net), as MET asks of mobile apps: Bunny acts on our behalf as a data processor under a data processing agreement, passes on the IP address and the rounded coordinates, and stores the answers for a short time so that MET does not have to be asked every time. At most once per hour per city, and only while you use the app. Legal basis: providing the service you requested (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f)). Terms: https://api.met.no/doc/TermsOfService

City search (Android Geocoder)

Searching for a city name, turning "Current location" into a city name and finding the country of a saved city (once per city, to show its local time) use your phone's built-in service; on most devices this is provided by Google Play services, so the search text or coordinates may be sent to Google.

Speech recognition

When you dictate, the audio is processed by your phone's speech recognition service (Google on most devices). Planstep only receives the recognised text and does not store the audio.

Trusted time (Google Play services)

To protect the streak against tampering, the app requests the exact time from Google Play services (TrustedTime). Planstep sends no personal data for this.

Google Play age signals (Play Age Signals)

Where the law requires it and Google Play already supports it (currently in Brazil and, in the United States, in Texas), Google Play gives the app your age range (for example 16–17), and for a parent-supervised account also whether your parent has approved the app's latest significant change. We use this only on your phone while the app is running (it is not stored or passed on): to set up ads (non-personalised ads under 18), and if the age range is under 16 or approval is missing, the app can't be used. Legal basis: legal obligation.

The website (GitHub Pages)

The website https://planstep-app.github.io is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (USA). It uses no cookies, analytics or ads; for security and troubleshooting, GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses in its own logs (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Legal basis: legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

What you share

If you share something (for example a calendar export, workout log or image) or send a bug report by email, it goes to the app and recipient you choose. A bug report goes to planstepbug@gmail.com and contains the app version, the Android version and the device model.

6. Backups and changing phones

  • Nothing is backed up to the cloud: Android's automatic cloud backup is turned off for Planstep.
  • When you move to a new phone (by cable or wireless transfer), your data can be transferred to it.
  • The weekly automatic backup and exports are saved only to the folder you choose; if that is a cloud folder, they go there.

7. Transfers outside the EEA

Google may process data outside the European Economic Area (for example in the United States) under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (for users in Switzerland, the Swiss–US Data Privacy Framework) and standard contractual clauses. MET Norway operates in Norway, within the EEA; Bunny (BunnyWay d.o.o.) is an EU company and serves requests from people in the EEA from its European servers. GitHub, Inc. (USA), which hosts the website, also processes visitors' IP addresses under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (for visitors from Switzerland, the Swiss–US Data Privacy Framework) and standard contractual clauses.

8. How long is data kept?

Data on your phone stays until you delete it or the app. Weather forecasts and hourly weather values are deleted after 2 days. Google and MET Norway keep the data they receive for the periods set out in their own policies; Bunny keeps its request logs, with the IP address anonymised, for at most 3 days.

You can change or withdraw your ad consent here: Settings → Privacy settings → Ad partners and consent management (outside the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, also with the Personalized ads switch there). For data held by Google: https://myaccount.google.com

You can enter your date of birth again only after Settings → Delete all data.
