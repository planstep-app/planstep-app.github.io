Planstep – Delete your data

Planstep app · Developer and data controller: CO-PRINT Kft. · Valid from: 11 November 2026

This page is about the Planstep app. It explains how you can delete your data, what is deleted and what is kept.

How to delete your data

Planstep has no account and no server of its own: what you enter in the app is stored only on your phone. So you can delete your data yourself, at once:

  • Open Planstep and tap the Settings button in the top right corner of the opening screen.
  • In the "Data" section tap "Delete all data" and confirm.
  • Uninstalling the app from your phone does the same.

If you want us to delete data

The only data that reaches us is what you yourself send us by e-mail (a question, a complaint or a bug report). To have these deleted:

  • Write to planstepcont@gmail.com from the e-mail address you used before.
  • Use this subject: Data deletion – Planstep.
  • We reply within 30 days and delete the e-mails we received from you.

What is deleted

  • "Delete all data" and uninstalling the app delete everything the app stores on your phone: events, reminders, notes and lists with their attachments, daily routines, goals, workouts, your streak, saved cities, settings, your date of birth and your choice about ads.
  • On request we delete the e-mails you sent us (questions, complaints, bug reports).

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups and exports that you saved to a folder of your choice: these are your own files, and you can delete them in your file manager.
  • Data that went to Google for ads (advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, ad interactions, consent status): Google keeps it for the periods set out in its own policy. You can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Google → Ads), and you can manage the data Google holds about you at https://myaccount.google.com
  • Data sent for the weather forecast (the rounded coordinates of your saved cities and your IP address): Bunny keeps its request logs, with the IP address anonymised, for at most 3 days; MET Norway keeps data for the period set out in its own policy. If you remove your saved cities, the app sends no more such requests.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep (for example the records of a complaint or of proceedings before an authority) is kept for the required period.
